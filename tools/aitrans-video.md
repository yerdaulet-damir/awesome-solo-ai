# AITrans.Video

AITrans.Video is an AI video translation and dubbing workspace for creators, built for long-form content. It turns one video into a translated, dubbed version with subtitles, and lets you review and correct every sentence before export.

- Website: https://aitrans.video/
- Free tier: free preview without signup, up to 30 minutes per video, watermarked.
- Costs: Free / Plus $16.99 per month / Pro $49.99 per month.
- Made by: solo founder Xiong Ge (Wuhan), founded 2026.

## Use it when

- You publish translated long-form talks, interviews or lessons and need the whole video translated, not just short clips.
- You want to check and fix the translation and dubbing sentence by sentence before anyone sees it.
- You already edit in CapCut or Jianying and want to keep the final edit in your own tool.

## Why choose it over Rask AI / HeyGen / ElevenLabs

- Built for long videos: a single video up to 180 minutes on Pro, instead of the short-clip limits typical of other dubbing tools.
- Sentence-by-sentence review: the Studio lets you review and correct the transcription, translation and dubbing line by line, and fix one line without re-exporting the whole video.
- Keeps your editing workflow: export editing drafts to CapCut / Jianying, so you can finish the video in the editor you already use.

## Example workflow

1. Upload your video and pick the target language. Auto-dubbing languages include Chinese, English, Portuguese, Japanese, Thai, Indonesian and Vietnamese.
2. The workspace auto-transcribes the video, translates it and rewrites the translation for voice.
3. AI dubbing generates the new voice track, with subtitle and voice alignment.
4. Open the Studio and review the video sentence by sentence. Correct a line of text or voice, and fix that one line without re-exporting the whole video.
5. Export the finished video, or export an editing draft to CapCut / Jianying and finish it there.

Try the flow first with the free preview: no signup, up to 30 minutes per video, watermarked.

## Cost

Free / Plus $16.99 per month / Pro $49.99 per month. A single video up to 180 minutes is on Pro; the free preview is limited to 30 minutes per video and is watermarked.

## Limitations

- Auto-dubbing covers Chinese, English, Portuguese, Japanese, Thai, Indonesian and Vietnamese. Languages outside that list are not covered by auto-dubbing.
- The free preview is watermarked and limited to 30 minutes per video.
- Long videos up to 180 minutes need a Pro plan.

## Alternatives

- [Rask AI](https://www.rask.ai/) — AI video translation and dubbing across 130+ languages, with voice cloning and lip-sync. Choose it if you need a language outside AITrans.Video's auto-dubbing list or a large language range.
- [HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com/) — AI video translation with lip-sync for training, marketing and product videos. Choose it if lip-synced translated video is the priority.
- [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/) — best-in-class dubbing for video, from $5/mo. Choose it if you want dubbing quality and voice choice above a review-and-edit workflow.

## Do not use it for

- Translating into a language outside its auto-dubbing list (Chinese, English, Portuguese, Japanese, Thai, Indonesian, Vietnamese).
- Publishing the free, watermarked preview as a finished video.
- Skipping review and expecting a finished translation without checking the Studio output first.

## Real evidence

The workflow above is the product's working workflow as described on its website: upload a video, auto-transcribe, translate, rewrite for voice, AI-dub, align subtitles and voice, then review sentence by sentence in the Studio and correct individual lines before export. The free preview lets you run that workflow on a video of up to 30 minutes without signup (watermarked), so you can verify the Studio review step before paying.
